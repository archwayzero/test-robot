# crypt_kit contract (run 1)

Owner: orchestrator. Consumers: modeler (builds the pieces), `maze` module (assembles them), `player` (clearances),
qa (budgets). Look reference: `reference/map/crypt.jpg` (Winchester Cathedral crypt): groin-vaulted bays,
semicircular transverse arches on short square piers with simple block capitals, warm sandstone ashlar, plastered
vault webs, worn flagstone floor. Do NOT model the statue in the photo (copyrighted artwork) and no standing water.

## Grid
- One maze cell = **4.0 m x 4.0 m**. `MazeLayout.cell_size_m() == 4.0`.
- Walls sit **on cell edges**, centred on the edge line, **0.6 m thick** (0.3 m into each cell). Clear corridor
  width = 3.4 m.
- Piers (0.6 x 0.6 m) stand on every cell **corner** that touches at least one wall or arch.
- Units: meters, +Y up in Godot, floor at y = 0.

## Heights (from floor)
| Element | Height |
|---|---|
| Pier shaft | 0.0 - 1.9 |
| Capital (block, slightly wider: 0.75 x 0.75) | 1.9 - 2.2 |
| Springing line (arches and vault start) | 2.2 |
| Transverse arch soffit crown (true semicircle, radius 1.7) | 3.9 |
| Vault web crown (semi-elliptical section allowed; spring 2.2) | 4.15 |
| Wall top, arch block top, ceiling insert top (meet vault) | 4.15 |

The 0.25 m between arch soffit crown and vault crown is deliberate: arch rings must stand proud of the webs along
their whole length, as in the reference. Changed from a shared 3.9 crown in T015, where a flush arch showed no ring.
Player clearances are unchanged. The `maze` greybox keeps its 3.9 `WALL_HEIGHT` until T018 swaps in the kit.

## Clearances (player collision, must match `player` module)
| Clearance | Free height | Who fits | Kit piece |
|---|---|---|---|
| FULL | >= 2.2 (under arches) | stand (hero height, 1.65-1.85) | `vault_bay` |
| CROUCH | 1.3 | crouch (1.0), crawl | `ceiling_crouch` |
| CRAWL | 0.75 | crawl (0.5) only | `ceiling_crawl` |
Stand-up checks in `player` rely on these: a CROUCH cell must block a standing capsule, a CRAWL cell a crouching one.

## Pieces (one GLB: `godot/assets/crypt_kit/model/crypt_kit.glb`, one root node per piece, names exact)
| Node name | Origin | Description |
|---|---|---|
| `floor` | cell centre, y=0 | 4 x 4 m flagstones, top at y=0, 0.2 m thick |
| `wall` | edge centre, y=0 | 4.0 m long (between pier faces 3.4 m + overlap), 0.6 thick, 0-4.15 m, ashlar |
| `pier` | corner, y=0 | 0.6 x 0.6 shaft + capital, 0-2.2 m |
| `arch` | edge centre, y=0 | transverse semicircular arch spanning an OPEN edge between two piers, soffit 2.2-3.9 m, block top 4.15 m, 0.6 deep |
| `vault_bay` | cell centre, y=0 | groin vault covering one cell (two intersecting vaults, springing 2.2, crown 4.15) |
| `ceiling_crouch` | cell centre, y=0 | low collapsed/rubble ceiling insert, underside at 1.3 m, fills to 4.15 m |
| `ceiling_crawl` | cell centre, y=0 | lower insert, underside at 0.75 m, fills to 4.15 m |
Each piece has a `-colonly` child (boxes / simple trimesh) for collision. Vault and arch collision may be coarse;
floor and walls must be exact planes at y=0 and at the wall faces.

## Materials and textures
- At most 2 materials for the whole kit: `stone_ashlar` (walls, piers, arches, low ceilings) and `stone_floor`
  (floor; vault webs may use either). Principled BSDF, procedural in Blender, **baked** to texture maps
  (base colour, normal, ORM) at 1024 or 2048 px, tileable. Texture budget: docs/qa/budgets.json `crypt_kit`.
- A second UV channel (UV2, non-overlapping) on every piece for lightmaps.

- Textures may be generated by script (numpy or nodes) and saved as image maps, as long as the result is plain
  Principled BSDF image textures. Only the ashlar band and floor must tile; arch ring and plaster are atlas regions.
- Exported import products (`.import`, extracted textures) live beside the GLB and belong to the modeler. VRAM
  compression must be enabled: the memory budget assumes ASTC on device.

## Vault web section
Webs are semi-elliptical with half-span up to 1.84 m. They spring at 2.2 behind wall and arch faces; the visible groin
lands at 2.90-3.05 above the capital.

## Rules for the assembler (`maze`, T018)
- Every edge of a vault cell holds a `wall` or an `arch`, including the 12 open edges between different clearances
  on the current seed. A missing arch shows the hidden web flare or opens a slot.
- Every interior corner gets a `pier`, including corners where walls run collinear.
- Do not rotate `floor` between cells. Alternate `wall` by 180 degrees (the two faces have different UV offsets) and
  apply a per-instance or world-space tint, because one wall mesh repeats every 4 m.
- Kit soiling is glTF vertex colour (COLOR_0). Godot ignores it unless the materials set
  `vertex_color_use_as_albedo = true`; check `vertex_color_is_srgb` against a reference render.
- Use anisotropic filtering on kit materials (`texture_filter` 5, linear mipmap anisotropic): the imported sampler is
  filter 3 and visibly blurs floors and walls at grazing angles.
- Keep lights near neutral: the albedo is already warm, and warm lamps push walls past the reference colour.
- `ceiling_crouch` and `ceiling_crawl` use the strongest COLOR_0 shading in the kit; check them under real game light.
- Review renders use 96 lights in Cycles. Judge the shipped look in a Mobile scene, not from those renders.
- Rely on culling: a full FULL-cell maze is about 730k tris (seed 20261025), against 300k per frame.

## wall_lamp (separate asset `godot/assets/wall_lamp/model/wall_lamp.glb`)
Wrought-iron bracket with an oil lamp/candle, origin at the wall mounting point, light emitter at local
(0, 0.25, 0.35). <= 1,000 triangles, 1 material + emissive flame. The `maze` module places it at `lamp_slots()` at
y = 2.0 and adds the light source; the GLB contains no lights.
