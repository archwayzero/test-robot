## Grey first frame
**A loading frame, with the wrong colour. Exactly 1 frame of 12 captured.**
- Frame 0 is uniform RGB (76, 76, 76), which is Godot's default clear colour 0.3. Frame 1 is the full game with the HUD.
- Likely cause, from the code: `_boot()` waits for the maze world's 2 physics frames, so no camera exists at the first render, and the game's near-black environment is not used without one.
- `ready_to_play` arrives 1 frame after load in the sandbox.
- The count on an iPhone is not measured. Maze load time may differ there.

## Findings
| Id | Severity | Finding | Suggested owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | cosmetic, design call | Hero disappears completely after stopping just past a low passage's mouth | `module-dev` player |
| F2 | cosmetic | Hero looks turned toward the camera for 8 frames after boot | `module-dev` player |
| F3 | cosmetic | First frame is mid grey, not near-black | orchestrator (`project.godot`) |

**F1.** Standing still up to about 0.6 m past the mouth, the camera arm is blocked behind the player. Measured after 90 idle frames, same at both passage types:

| Distance past low-cell centre | Camera to body | Fade |
|---|---|---|
| 2.3 m | 0.16 m | 1.000 (hidden) |
| 2.6 m | 0.48 m | 1.000 (hidden) |
| 3.0 m | 0.90 m | 0.000 |
| 4.0 m | 1.96 m | 0.000 |

The hero stays hidden while the player stands there. Walking about 1 m past the mouth brings it back. Seen in the video at 00:15 and 00:29. The view ahead stays clear, so this follows the fade rule, but a human should decide if a vanishing hero is acceptable.

**F2.** `player.gd` `_update_fade()` starts with fade 0.181 on frame 0 and reaches 0 on frame 8, although the camera is 1.64 m away. The body does not rotate: yaw stays -1.571. The likely cause is a stale camera position on the first physics frame. While the alpha material copies are on, the far side of the mesh shows through, so the hero looks turned around, then snaps back. The same copies are used for every partial fade, so low-ceiling fades may show this too (not checked). `test_m2_boot_frames` prints this number and does not assert it, so the suite stays green with F2 open.

**F3.** Suggested fix: set `rendering/environment/defaults/default_clear_color` near the environment's Color(0.015, 0.014, 0.02). Not applied: outside qa scope.

## Deviations
- Video uses `--mode fast` (Compatibility renderer, OpenGL on llvmpipe), so lighting and materials differ from the iOS renderer. Mobile cost about 1 s per frame, which does not fit 170 s per call.
- Video is four recordings, each a fresh boot, joined with ffmpeg. The first 0.4 s of parts 2-4 is trimmed, which removes their grey frame. Part 1 keeps its boot frames. The cuts at 00:07.6, 00:16.2 and 00:23.3 are not continuous play; the 00:23.3 cut restarts inside the CRAWL cell.
- The route test uses actions, not touch. Touch stays covered by E14 and E15.
- Injected saved stances are written to `Core.state` before `save_now()`: a real save from the current game would not hold them. Save tests enter the passage by `spawn_at` from the neighbouring cell.
- Beyond the public API, for reading only: the hero and kit checks walk the node tree, and the fade reading uses `get_visual_fade()` by `call`. Asked hook: none needed yet, but `get_visual_fade()` could go public if F2 gets an assertion.
- Two temporary probe tests were used for F1 and F2, then deleted. The F1 table comes from the probe, not from a committed test.
- `tools/qa/capture.py` reported CPU rendering (llvmpipe), no OpenGL fallback.
